# BillingCenter — Workflows, Rules & Test Standards

## Account & Policy Billing Setup

### Billing Account Creation Rules
- One billing account per named insured (by default; configurable)
- Account number format: `BA` + 8 digits
- Billing method options: `Direct Bill`, `Agency Bill`, `List Bill`
- Default payment plan set at account level; can be overridden per policy

### Invoice Generation Rules
- Invoices generated by scheduled batch `InvoiceDueDateBatch`
- Invoice lead time: 21 days before due date (configurable per plan)
- Invoice format: paper, email, or both — set per account
- Invoice number format: `INV` + 10 digits, unique per account

### Payment Plans
| Plan Code | Frequency | Down Payment | Installments |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| `FULL_PAY` | Annual | 100% | 0 |
| `SEMI_ANNUAL` | 6 months | 50% | 1 |
| `QUARTERLY` | 3 months | 25% | 3 |
| `MONTHLY_10` | Monthly | 10% | 9 |
| `EFT_MONTHLY` | Monthly (EFT) | 0% | 12 |

## Payment Processing

### Accepted Payment Methods
- Check (manual entry or lockbox)
- EFT / ACH
- Credit card (via payment gateway integration)
- Wire transfer (commercial accounts only)

### Payment Application Rules
- Payments applied oldest-due-first by default (`FIFO` algorithm)
- Suspense account used when payment cannot be matched to account
- Unapplied payments held in suspense for 30 days before return
- Overpayment: refund issued if credit > $5 (configurable threshold `MinRefundAmount`)

### NSF (Non-Sufficient Funds) Rules
- NSF fee applied per policy terms (typical: $25)
- NSF reverses the payment and marks invoice `Past Due`
- 2nd NSF within 12 months triggers `Delinquency` workflow
- EFT enrollment suspended after NSF until reinstated by insured

## Delinquency Workflow

### Delinquency States
`Current → Past Due → Delinquent → Cancellation Pending → Cancelled`

### Delinquency Thresholds (Configurable)
- Past Due: 1 day after due date
- Delinquent: 10 days past due (notice sent)
- Cancellation Pending: 20 days past due (cancellation notice sent to PolicyCenter)
- Cancellation executed: 30 days past due

### Delinquency Notices
| Notice | Trigger | Template |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Reminder Notice | 1 day past due | `BC_REMINDER_001` |
| Delinquency Notice | 10 days | `BC_DELINQUENCY_001` |
| Cancellation Warning | 20 days | `BC_CANCEL_WARNING_001` |

### Cure (Reinstatement) Rules
- Payment of full past-due balance cures delinquency automatically
- Partial payment: delinquency holds; curing threshold = 100% of past-due amount
- Cure during Cancellation Pending: cancellation request to PolicyCenter is rescinded

## Agency Bill

### Agency Bill Cycle
1. Monthly statement generated for agency account
2. Agency remits net payment (gross premium minus commission)
3. Reconciliation matches remittance to individual policies
4. Discrepancies go to `Agency Bill Suspense` for manual resolution

### Testing Notes for Agency Bill
- Test short remittance: agency pays less than statement total
- Test over-remittance: agency pays more
- Test late remittance: past statement due date
- Test commission clawback on cancelled policy mid-cycle

## BillingCenter Testing Checklist

- Payment plan change mid-term: verify pro-rated installment recalculation
- NSF: verify fee applied, invoice reversal, EFT suspension
- Delinquency progression: verify each notice triggers at correct day threshold
- Overpayment refund: verify $5 threshold (amounts below = credit hold, above = refund)
- FIFO payment application: verify oldest invoice paid first with split payment
- Suspense: verify 30-day hold then return for unmatched payments
- Agency bill: short remittance → suspense → manual resolution workflow
- EFT monthly plan: verify 0% down, 12 equal installments, ACH schedule creation
